      The Cardinal UPI Security Rule

      Entering your UPI PIN always DEBITS money from your bank account. You never need to enter your UPI PIN, scan a QR code, or approve a collect request in order to receive money or cashback rewards. If someone asks you to enter your PIN to receive funds, it is guaranteed fraud.

      1930 Cybercrime Golden Hour

      If you have fallen victim to an unauthorized debit, dial the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 immediately. Reporting within the first 2 hours ("Golden Hour") drastically increases the likelihood of freezing the stolen funds across nodal bank accounts.

      Electricity Bill Disconnection

      Scammers send fake SMS claiming power will be cut tonight at 9:30 PM. They provide a personal 10-digit number and ask you to install an APK file (AnyDesk) to steal banking credentials.

      Fake Bank KYC Deactivation

      Messages claiming your YONO SBI or NetBanking account is suspended due to PAN non-compliance. Links lead to cloned phishing portals that capture your login ID, password, and OTP.

      OLX / Marketplace Advance Scam

      Buyer poses as an Army officer or genuine buyer, agrees to your item price without bargaining, and sends a "Collect Request" pretending to pay advance money.
